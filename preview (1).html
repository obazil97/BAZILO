How I work

I do not start with content. I start with the problem.

My working model is simple: research enough to understand the context, diagnose the real constraint, choose a strategic response, execute with discipline, then learn from the signal.

01

Research

Audience, channel, category, competitors, current messaging and observable performance.

02

Diagnose

Separate symptoms from the actual problem. What is preventing attention, trust, intent or conversion?

03

Strategize

Define the positioning, communication job, content pillars, campaign idea and KPI logic.

04

Execute

Turn the strategy into calendars, copy, briefs, community actions, creative and distribution.

05

Measure

Look beyond vanity metrics. Ask what changed and whether the content moved the intended behavior.

06

Learn

Convert performance and audience feedback into reusable learning rather than starting from zero.

07

Optimize

Stop, start, continue, fix or test, based on evidence.

08

Repeat

Strong marketing compounds because every campaign improves the next decision.
